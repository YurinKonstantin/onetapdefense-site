Privacy Policy

1. In short

The game requires no registration, asks for no personal data and does not send information about you to the developer's servers. Your progress is stored on your device only.

External services are used only where needed to show optional advertising or to process purchases through an app store. If advertising is shown in your build, the ad network may receive the device advertising identifier. Payments and card data are processed by the app store, not by the game.

2. What data the app processes

2.1. Data that stays on your device

Game progress is saved locally in the app's private directory: gold, experience and level, best score and rating across the ten sectors, which sectors are unlocked, ad-removal entitlements and purchase records, kill and build counters, daily login and quest state. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

2.2. Data an ad service may receive

If advertising is shown in your build (a banner in the menu or a rewarded video), the VK Ads network may receive: the device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID or the platform's equivalent), the IP address and the region derived from it, device model and operating system version, system language, that an ad was served and viewed, and technical ad-loading events.

Watching advertising is optional: you can decline it and the game stays fully playable.

2.3. Payment data

The game offers optional purchases: packs of in-game gold and ad removal. Payments are processed by the app store (RuStore, Microsoft Store): card data, the payment token and payment confirmation are handled by the store and are not passed to the game or to the developer. The game receives only technical purchase information — the product identifier and the transaction identifier — and stores the identifier of a confirmed purchase on the device to avoid granting gold twice.

2.4. Data the app does not collect

The game does not request or transmit: your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, device files, the list of installed apps, microphone or camera data. There are no accounts, no analytics SDKs, no in-game behaviour trackers and no user profiling.

3. Why the data is processed

  • Local game data — to keep your progress between sessions.
  • Ad service data — to show advertising and grant the reward for watching a video (continuing a run, doubling gold). Watching advertising is optional: you can decline it and the game stays fully playable.
  • Purchase data (product and transaction identifiers) — to deliver purchased gold, enable ad removal and restore a purchase after reinstalling. Purchases are optional: the game can be completed without them.

The legal basis for processing is your consent given by action: launching the app and, for advertising, viewing an ad.

4. Third parties

Advertising is delivered by VK Ads, operated by VK (LLC “VK”). Data processing on the ad service side is governed by its own documents:

  • VK Privacy Policy: vk.com/privacy
  • VK Ads platform rules and documents: ads.vk.com

Payments are accepted by the app stores (RuStore, Microsoft Store), which process payments and payment data under their own documents.

  • RuStore legal documents and privacy policy: rustore.ru/help/legal
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement: privacy.microsoft.com

No data is shared with anyone else. The developer does not receive the data that the ad service and the store collect on their own, and never receives card data.

5. Children

The game is rated 12+. It is not intended for children under 12 and does not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child's data has reached the ad service, write to yurinkonstantin@mail.ru — we will help and, if needed, explain how to reset the advertising identifier on the device.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • delete all local data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the device settings;
  • reset the advertising identifier and limit ad personalisation in the device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; Windows: Settings → Privacy → General);
  • ask a question about data processing at yurinkonstantin@mail.ru.

Requests are reviewed within 30 days.

7. Storage and security

Local data is stored in the app's private directory, accessible only to the game itself, and never leaves the device. Data sent to the ad service travels over HTTPS. Retention periods on the ad service and store side are defined by their own documents.

8. App permissions

The app needs internet access — to load ad creatives and to process purchases through the app store. For optional purchases, the Android build declares the billing permission (com.android.vending.BILLING); it only allows sending a purchase request to the store and grants no access to the device or to payment data. No other permissions are requested.

The game is fully playable offline: ads are not loaded, purchases and purchase restoration are unavailable, and progress is stored locally.

9. Changes to this policy

The current version of this policy is always available at yurinkonstantin.github.io/onetapdefense-site/privacy-en.html. For material changes the effective date at the top of the document is updated.

10. Contact

For any questions about data processing: yurinkonstantin@mail.ru
