Bulwark: Sector 9

Ten sectors. Five towers. One line you cannot give up. Enemies march along the route to your base — place and upgrade towers, pan the map with your finger and hold wave after wave.

Holding the line

A portrait tower defense: one hand, a map wider than the screen, and every sector with its own panorama and route.

01 · Towers

Five towers, three tiers

The rifleman fires fast and hits ground and air. Frost slows enemies by 35%. The cannon deals splash damage to ground units only. Tesla chains through three targets. Poison deals damage over time and cracks tanks. Every tower upgrades to tier three.

02 · Enemies

A swarm: infantry, runners, tanks

Six enemy types: infantry, fast runners, armoured tanks, birds flying straight over the road, spitters that jam towers, and bosses. A boss appears every tenth wave and at the end of each sector.

03 · Maps

Ten sectors

East Gate, Concrete Yard, Fuel Depot, Cargo Terminal, Salt Quarry, Blizzard Slope, Reactor Loop, Ore Bridge, Frozen Dock and the Dust Core. Biomes: asphalt, concrete, steel, sand, snow and rock.

04 · Controls

The map under your thumb

The panorama is wider than the screen: drag to pan, pinch to zoom into a chokepoint or pull back to the whole field. Lightning strike — three charges per wave, area damage.

05 · Sessions

Ten to twenty minutes

One sector is a single short run, and you can minimise the game at any moment. Three daily quests, a daily login, and gold and experience between runs.

06 · Data

No sign-up

Progress is saved locally on your device and is never sent to our servers. The game is fully playable without an internet connection. See the Privacy Policy for details.
